Hilbert was slow to grasp math as per the biography but he was stubborn to figure things out and most importantly knew the boundary of his ignorance — a rare and difficult quality to possess in modern day teaching contexts. 

It reminds me of Feynman's quote about how easy it is to fool your own self and that's what one should aim to prevent. 

Realised after so much of teaching how badly social media & phones have affected youngsters, they don't possess the attention span to hold a proper convo! let alone self-awareness, responsibility (upon one's self), etc...

I realised teaching in a flawed system is thus rewarding nonetheless. Just as my teacher made me realise my path, I could be a good influence to at least some which could propagate & affect the lives of many and in an excellent way, I hope.
