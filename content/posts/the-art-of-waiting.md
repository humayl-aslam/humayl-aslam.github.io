---

As it ticks past 11:03 AM & I head on towards the waiting spot, I skim the whereabouts quickly and spot an old man (in his 80s/90s?) waiting at the MTR exit with a pink bouquet of flowers. The bouquet looks gorgeous & fresh and I stop to wonder (not literally stop, but in my mind) how beautiful is the moment that I just witnessed. 

As I write this, the couple is walking towards me with a pair of identical pink coloured flower filled bouquets 💐. It’s a wonderful sight and a rather rare one. 

My mind starts to wander off into a question posed by a friend some time ago and it can be summarised as: *“we have forgotten the art of waiting”*. This is truer now than ever. 

While I casually tap my shoe, purse my lips, and look around in the train I cannot help but notice the constant urge for stimulus in people of all kind and people of all ages. Why is it that we have forgotten to wait and just wait patiently. 

Perhaps it is our constant attachment to mobile phones and some sort of stimulus that just does not let us sit idly with our thoughts. Perhaps it’s crowd behaviour, if some people use it, we ought to use it. Perhaps it’s to get out of social awkwardness. 

But what’s more comforting than just waiting and entertaining your thoughts. 

Every now often, mustn’t it be healthy, quite healthy to get a pink bouquet, full of fresh flowers, clasp it in both hands and wait. 

Just wait.
